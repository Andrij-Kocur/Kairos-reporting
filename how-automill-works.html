How Automill Works

Automill is the Kairos system that runs a feed mill from truck to loadout. Every batch is weighed, mixed, pelleted and recorded.

Intake

Every load checked in, weighed and recorded.

  • Truck weights feed straight into the stock record
  • Scalper and magnet protect the hammer mills and pellet dies
  • Dust aspiration keeps the intake pit clean

Storage and routing

The right ingredient in the right bin, every time.

  • One ingredient per bin, so no cross-contamination
  • Gates and diverters are position-proven before conveyors start
  • Level switches stop filling and warn batching

Grinding

Two hammer mills, held at their best load.

  • Feeder speed keeps the mill motor at 85–95% of rated current
  • Vibration, temperature and door interlocks protect the mill
  • Mills run in parallel, so one can be serviced while the other grinds

Batching

Accurate to the kilogram, formula after formula.

  • Fast fill then slow dribble, with early stop for material in flight
  • Hand-adds are scanned and must match the recipe before they are added
  • The next batch is weighed while the mixer runs

Mixing

Uniform feed in every batch.

  • Twin-shaft paddle mixing in 60–120 seconds
  • Oil, molasses, methionine and choline dosed by flow meter
  • Low-residue discharge limits carry-over between formulas

Pelleting

Steam, pressure and heat under control.

  • Conditioner temperature controls the steam valve
  • Feeder speed sets the throughput of each line
  • Automatic greasing and blockage protection for the press

Cooling and crumbling

Cooled, dried and sized.

  • Counterflow cooling to within about 5 °C of ambient
  • Temperature probes watch for hot spots
  • Crumbler sizes feed for young animals and the screener returns fines

Fat coating

Energy added after the press.

  • Heated oil dosed by pump and mass flow meter
  • 1–5% fat added with accuracy around ±1%
  • Also applies heat-sensitive enzymes and vitamins

Loadout and bagging

From bin to truck or bag, on the record.

  • The system records which batch is in which bin
  • Trucks are weighed as they fill, so delivered mass matches the order
  • 25, 40 or 50 kg bags with checkweigher and metal detector

Control and traceability

One system from the weighbridge to the loadout lane.

  • Conveyor routes start last-first and stop in flow order
  • Speed monitors, plug switches and gates are interlocked, so a fault stops everything upstream
  • Every batch is recorded for traceability
