Control systems and SCADA

PLC design and programming

The control sequence sets out what the equipment should do, the conditions it needs to run and what must stop it. Kairos develops the control narratives, PLC architecture, interlocks and alarms for new systems and upgrades.

Operator screens and alarms

HMI and SCADA show the status of instruments, motors and valves. In Automill's batching workflow, recipe and order information also follows each batch through its holding points.

Testing and commissioning

The work includes factory acceptance testing, cold and hot commissioning, fault finding and control-system records for the plant's maintenance team.

Electrical and Instrumentation

Design and panel manufacture

Concept, feasibility and detailed electrical design lead into MCC manufacturing, PLC panels and remote I/O. The site work includes electrical construction, field cabling, installation and commissioning.

Instrumentation

Temperature, vibration and acoustic measurements give the control system information about the process. Kairos selects and installs the sensors, process instruments and signal conditioning for those measurements.

Checks on site

Loop checks and equipment checks establish how the installed instruments and equipment respond. Kairos checks signal behaviour and installation quality during commissioning and records the results.

Networks and data acquisition

Industrial networks

Kairos designs, installs and tests the connections between controllers, field devices, computers and supervisory systems. The work includes network architecture and integration between industrial protocols.

Data acquisition

PC-based data acquisition captures measurements from instruments and sensors for logging. Kairos uses National Instruments DAQ hardware and provides pulse and frequency measurement alongside OPC integration.

Industrial computers

Industrial PCs and interface computers run measurement, logging and operator software on the plant. Hardware selection takes account of the installation environment and support requirements.

Vision, software and specialist systems

Vision systems

Kairos integrates camera inspection into the plant control sequence using NI Vision Builder for Automated Inspection. Camera selection follows the resolution and inspection speed the application needs.

Industrial software

Kairos writes LabVIEW software and reviews code. It also configures and integrates third-party hardware and software, with hardware specification, project management and end-user training.

Data interfaces and notifications

OPC and SQL interfaces connect industrial software with plant data. SMS and GSM notifications provide a route for plant-event messages to reach the people handling them.
